### Defects / remaining checks

1. **Reserved routing area must be accounted for.**  
   Page 16 explicitly says **“Reserved area: Please do not route tracks between pads.”** A footprint containing only the listed pads, body, ports and courtyard omits this requirement. Add an appropriate front-copper track keepout/rule area between the lands, or enforce and document it at board level. Do not turn the entire footprint into a copper prohibition that disallows its own pads; the source specifically prohibits tracks in the reserved area.

2. **Zero mask/paste is a provisional process setting, not a source-derived recommendation.**  
   Your stated qualification is correct. However, **verify the effective KiCad settings and generated mask/paste apertures**: zero local margin values can mean inheritance from footprint/board settings rather than a forced zero expansion. Paste ratio settings can also affect the result. If your intent is exactly pad-sized apertures, verify that outcome explicitly.

3. **Provide an unambiguous pin-1 indicator.**  
   If not already present, add a Fab pin-1 mark and an appropriate visible assembly/silkscreen indicator. The diagonal cap ports alone are not a reliable pin-1 indicator because their pattern is invariant under a 180° rotation.

**Bottom line:** no correction is needed to the proposed land geometry, numbering, port coordinates, or courtyard. Address the reserved-area requirement and verify the effective manufacturing-layer settings before release.
